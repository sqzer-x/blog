Hyukjin An — Software & Security Engineer, and a founder, based in South Korea.
Most places online I go by **SQZER**, because I like squeezing a thing down until it
breaks and shows me what it was made of. Squeezer is fine too.

I like having a hand in the whole lifecycle of a service, from the hardware and the
systems underneath it through DevSecOps and on to planning and writing the thing. That
has taken me through enterprise infrastructure, SysOps, full-stack development and
security engineering; these days I work as a software engineer inside the security
domain. I started out as a developer, so my languages are C++, Rust and Python, and I
spend a lot of my open-source time building the tools security testing actually needs
and contributing to other people's. Right now most of my attention goes to OPSEC and
zero trust.

The rest of my time goes to running my own things. As a solo founder that means
reviewing the services I already have, launching new ones, and doing every other job
myself — finding the business model, handling the legal side, working out how anyone is
supposed to hear about it.

Hardware is part of the fun. My first homelab ran on a Dell PowerEdge R650; now it is a
Lenovo ThinkStation P3 Tower under Proxmox. Day to day I work on a ThinkPad X1 Carbon
Gen 13 Aura Edition running Arch Linux, in Ghostty and NeoVIM, with Codex CLI and Claude
Code alongside.

generator that builds [sqzer.com](https://sqzer.com/). There is more on
